# Third-party notices

## Scope

The seven notebooks under `notebooks/` contain coursework adaptations of the University of Manchester COMP34212 Keras CIFAR CNN laboratory foundation. Retained elements include the Sequential convolution/pooling/dropout/dense pattern, preprocessing, evaluation and plotting idioms. The author extended this foundation to CIFAR-100 and the experiments described in the README. These notices apply to the retained upstream portions across all seven notebooks.
